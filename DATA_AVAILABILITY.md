# Data Availability

The materials publicly available in this repository include:

- platform source code;
- the guided-use protocol and student-facing materials;
- orientation slides;
- productive and unproductive prompt examples;
- the transcript-coding codebook;
- selected de-identified interaction excerpts;
- study and model configuration information.

The following study materials are not publicly released:

- full raw chat transcripts;
- participant-level assessment data;
- student identifiers and account records;
- original group-assignment files;
- uploaded answer sheets.

These materials are not publicly released because they contain learner interaction
and assessment data subject to privacy and consent constraints.

The publicly released reproducibility materials are intended to document the
guided-use intervention, coding framework, interaction examples, and study
configuration without exposing identifiable or participant-level study data.
